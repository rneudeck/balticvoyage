💡 Good to Know

  • Emergency: dial 112 across all six countries.
  • Daylight: late June = ~18 hrs of light — perfect for long outdoor dinners.
  • Shoes: cobblestones in Visby, Tallinn, Riga & Gdansk — bring your sturdiest pair.
  • Amber: Gdansk is the world capital of amber — Mariacka St. is the place to buy.
  • Reservations: book Vasa Museum & Helsinki’s Savoy online in advance.
